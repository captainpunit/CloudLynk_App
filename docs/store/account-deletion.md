# Delete your CloudLynk account

> Draft for the client. Google Play requires a public web page with this content; put its
> URL in Play Console → App content → Data safety → "Delete account URL". Fill every
> `[bracket]`.

CloudLynk is published by [legal business name].

## In the app

1. Open CloudLynk and sign in.
2. Go to **You → Settings → Delete account**.
3. Enter your password, type `DELETE`, and confirm.

## Without the app

Email [support email] from the address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my
account". We delete the account within [7] days and reply when it is done.

## What is deleted

- Your profile, email address and sign-in details
- Channels and videos you uploaded
- Files in My cloud
- Channels you joined, watch history and saved videos
- Notification tokens and notifications

## What is kept

- Payment records (plan, amount, date, UPI reference or Google Play order ID), without
  your name or email, for [8 years] because tax law requires it.
- Records of content removed for breaking the rules, where the law requires them.

Deleting an account cannot be undone. Time left on a paid plan is not refunded.
