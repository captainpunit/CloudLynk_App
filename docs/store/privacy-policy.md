CloudLynk ("the app") is run by [legal business name], [registered address], India
("we"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, why, and what you can
do about it. Contact: [support email].

## What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email address, display name, password (stored only as a hash) | You create an account | To sign you in and show your name |
| Videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions | A creator uploads them | To publish them in channels |
| Files in My cloud | You upload them | To store them privately for you |
| Channels you joined, watch progress, videos you saved | You use the app | To show your library and resume playback |
| Payment records: plan, amount, date, UPI reference number (UTR), order reference | You pay by UPI | To confirm your payment and start Plus |
| Your state in India | You pay by UPI in the Google Play version | Google requires it for tax reporting of that payment |
| Google Play purchase token and order ID | You pay with Google Play billing | To confirm the purchase with Google |
| Push notification token | You allow notifications | To send you notifications |
| Reports you send about a video or channel | You report content | To review it |
| Install source (Play install referrer), app version, device model and OS version | First start | To know which advertisement or link led to an install |
| Advertising ID and app events (app opened, plans viewed, checkout started) | Only if you tap "Allow" on the ad measurement question | To measure our Meta (Facebook, Instagram) advertisements |
| Purchase events (plan value, time) sent to Meta from our server | Only if you allowed ad measurement | Same purpose |

We do not collect your contacts, location, microphone or camera, and we never see your
UPI PIN, bank account or card number. UPI payments happen inside your own UPI app.

## Who we share it with

We use these companies to run the app. They process data on our behalf or as required to
provide their service:

- **Supabase** — database, sign-in and private file storage.
- **Cloudflare** — video storage and streaming.
- **Google Play** — billing, and reports of UPI payments made in the Play version.
- **Expo** and **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** — delivery of push notifications.
- **Meta Platforms** — advertising measurement, only with your permission.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose it to authorities only when the law requires.

## Ad measurement choice

The app asks once whether you allow ad measurement. If you decline, the Meta SDK is not
started and no purchase events about you are sent to Meta. You can change your choice at
any time in **You → Settings**.

## How long we keep it

- Account data, uploads and files: until you delete them or your account.
- Payment records: [8 years, or the period your accountant advises], because tax law
  requires it. After account deletion they are kept without your name or email.
- Server logs: up to 30 days.

## Deleting your account

In the app: **You → Settings → Delete account**. Without the app: see
[account deletion page URL]. Deletion removes your profile, uploads, files, watch history
and notification tokens. It cannot be undone, and time left on a paid plan is not refunded.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, correct it, withdraw consent for ad measurement,
or delete your account. Write to [support email]; we reply within [30] days.

## Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Videos in paid channels are streamed with
short-lived signed links. Access to the database is restricted by per-row rules.
